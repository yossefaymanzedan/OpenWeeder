2. Auto label

SAM finds every plant and Muse Spark calls it crop or weed using what this crop learned in its steps. Only confident calls become labels; unsure plants are left out. It uses the AI service and costs about $0.02 per image.

Small YOLO (yolo11n), at most {{ max_epochs }} epochs, on this computer. Numbers update after every epoch. They are measured on 20% of the AI-labeled images, so they show agreement with the AI's labels, not with a person.
