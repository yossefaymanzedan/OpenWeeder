Train a new crop with the robot

This is how a crop will be taught once the robot exists. Nothing on this page runs yet. For now, teach a crop with tray photos from New crop.

What will happen

  1. 1
    Drive three columns

    The robot drives down three columns of this crop once each, taking a photo every few centimeters. Nothing is sprayed.

  2. 2
    The AI labels, you review

    SAM and Muse Spark label every plant as crop or weed. You fix the mistakes.

  3. 3
    Train the offline model

    A small detector learns your reviewed labels on a computer, without the internet.

  4. 4
    Use it in a field

    Pick the trained crop in New field. The robot then detects and sprays in one pass.
