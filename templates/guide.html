PHOTO GUIDE

How to take field photos

The AI can only judge what it can see clearly. Good photos mean fewer mistakes for you to fix.

STAGE PROGRESS Step 2 of 4

Do and don't

● DO: Shoot straight down
PASS

Hold the phone flat, pointing straight down at the row.

The AI sees each plant's leaf shape best from directly above.

● DON'T: Cast your shadow on the plants
FAIL
block
SHADOW · SIMULATED

Stand so your shadow falls outside the photo.

Dark shadows hide leaves and make plants hard to tell apart from soil.

● DO: Natural daylight, no flash
PASS

Use daylight. Turn the flash off.

Flash makes bright shiny spots on leaves.

● DON'T: Angled perspective or motion blur
FAIL
motion_blur
BLURRY · SIMULATED

Keep the phone level and still. Take each photo at a different spot along the row.

Tilted or blurry photos make plants look the wrong shape. The upload page warns you about blurry or dark photos.

Example photos: CWFID carrot field dataset (Haug & Ostermann, 2014). The two “don't” examples are simulated from a real photo.
