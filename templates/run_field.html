2. Robot

Stop and go: the robot stops, takes a picture (cut to the tray), waits for the detection, sprays the weeds in that picture (backing up a little for weeds the 8 cm arm cannot reach from where it stopped), then moves one picture on (about {{ picture_cm or 28 }} cm) and repeats. A weed at a picture's front edge waits for the next picture, in case a crop grows just beyond it. The pump is a dummy pin: the log and video show where it would have sprayed. It covers at most {{ field_limit or 200 }} cm per run. For calibration, the arm can stay aimed at each weed for some seconds before the pump, so you can see where the nozzle really points (0 = no wait). Every run is saved below as a video with a summary.
